Same shape as mockup 08, t3-skinned. Spine cards are dark zinc panels with white-low-alpha borders; the host card carries a soft warm gradient. Sticky notes are tinted glass — each author color tinting a translucent card with a matching glow shadow. Pill chips throughout for tags, reactions, and footer actions. Subtle SVG noise overlay tints the entire surface.

What the t3 skin buys
  • The transcript becomes the brightest thing on screen by default — less chrome to compete
  • Author colors pop harder against zinc-950 than they did on cream — identity stays legible
  • Pill chips for reactions read as quick, low-stakes — matches whiteboard’s "casual" register
  • Glow shadows on sticky notes give the “sticker on glass” feel without paper-y kitsch
  • DM Sans lets prose, mono code, and meta labels coexist without serif weirdness
Watch out for
  • Saturated emoji can fight the monochrome palette — consider a "low-saturation emoji set" mode
  • Long sessions on near-black are eye-fatiguing without a light-mode toggle
  • Glass sticky notes are quieter than paper ones — if the user wants more “chat energy,” bump the glow opacity
  • Watcher palette needs accessibility check — especially mira lavender on dark for color-blind users

Design tokens lifted from t3.codes

Background #09090b · foreground #fafafa · muted #a1a1aa · dim #71717a · borders rgba(255,255,255,0.08). Typography is DM Sans 400/500/600 with optical-size axis 9–40, paired with JetBrains Mono for code/meta. Tagline letter-spacing -0.035em, headings weight 500. Hero pill button at border-radius: 999px with 0 0 24px #ffffff14 on hover. Fade-up entrance animations: 1s ease-out, staggered 0.05s/0.15s/0.25s. The signature is the inline SVG feTurbulence noise overlay at 3.5% opacity, fixed-positioned across the viewport.
