I've reviewed the privacy section. Three things stand out as missing:

  1. Comment storage policy. Comments persist in transcript history once posted. If a watcher pastes a secret into a comment, it's there forever. The section should address this.
  2. Redaction filter punted to v1.5 without specifics. The section names common patterns (API_KEY=, AWS keys) but doesn't enumerate them. Without a v1 default list, "defer to v1.5" can drift indefinitely.
  3. Display name leakage. Watchers pick display names that may include emails or real names. There's no policy on whether to allow plain usernames only, or how kicked users' names persist in the transcript.
